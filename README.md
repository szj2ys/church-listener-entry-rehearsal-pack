# Church listener entry rehearsal pack

An offline rehearsal for welcome teams helping people reach an existing church translation service without scanning a QR code. It checks a text link, a typed address, the correct service and language, a first received test passage, and a real fallback for someone who cannot use the phone route.

This is a free set of editable Markdown, plain-text cards and CSV worksheets. No software installation, account, payment or PewTranslate service is needed to use the documents. Testing a live listener route does require the translation provider and network your church actually uses.

## Use the pack

1. Copy [the operator handoff](templates/operator-handoff.txt) and obtain today's public listener address from the operator. Never substitute an administrator URL.
2. Complete [the listener card](templates/listener-card.txt). Replace every bracketed instruction, remove unsupported offers, and ask a competent reader to review any translated wording.
3. Follow [the rehearsal](docs/rehearsal.md) with an operator and a volunteer who did not prepare the card. Use [the blank log](templates/rehearsal-log.csv) to record observations and retests.
4. Use [the decision guide](docs/decision-guide.md) to distinguish a failed link from a wrong service, missing language, stopped text or unavailable sound.
5. Apply [the release check](docs/release-check.md) before printing or distributing the card. A page opening is not evidence that translation is arriving.

You can print the text files or paste them into your usual document editor. Use selectable text and a readable size; do not shrink the card to fit a preselected paper size. Offer the format the intended reader requests. The pack does not certify accessibility or replace testing with the people who will use your instructions.

## Worked examples

[The example handoff and card](examples/fictional-handoff-and-card.txt) and [the example log](examples/fictional-rehearsal-log.csv) show a wrong-service failure, a correction and a retest. Every observation is fictional. The `.invalid` example addresses deliberately do not work and must never be distributed to visitors.

The examples do not provide approved translations, interpreting, a worship order, or evidence of a congregation's results. Keep participant names, credentials, prayer requests and transcripts out of the worksheet. Store completed operational copies privately; the templates can remain public.

## What this adds

This pack covers the visitor's entry path and the handoff between welcome volunteer and operator. It is not a service-coverage planner, a glossary checker, a sign-design tool, a caption-layout player or a delay measurement tool. It can be used alongside those tools without copying their records.

For broader preparation, [PewTranslate's free guide to welcoming multilingual visitors](https://pewtranslate.com/en/guides/welcoming-multilingual-visitors) discusses planning language support. That optional background link is not required to complete this pack.
